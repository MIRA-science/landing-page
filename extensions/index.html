What people are trying outside the core.

MIRA's core schema is kept small on purpose. People who need more than it holds build extensions and try them in their own tools.

This page lists those extensions beside the use cases people have brought. Add yours, give a thumbs up to what you need, and find who to talk to.

Every extension listed here is an experiment run by the people its card names. None has been adopted into MIRA's core.
