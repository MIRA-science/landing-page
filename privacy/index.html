What we collect, and why.

This site keeps as little about you as it can. Here is all of it, in plain words.

Email updates

If you sign up for updates, we keep your email address, the date you signed up, and which signup form you used. We use it only to send occasional news about MIRA. To stop receiving updates, reply to any of them or write to us, and we will remove you.

Visiting the site

We count page visits with Vercel Web Analytics, which does not use cookies and does not identify you. The pages load their fonts from Google Fonts, so Google sees the request from your browser.

The library

The library is a members-only area for MIRA collaborators. Accounts are by invitation only. For each member we keep an email address and a name. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address, and profile picture with us, and nothing else.

Recordings, transcripts, and other files that members upload, and the descriptions they add, are stored privately and can be seen only by signed-in library members. Your browser keeps your sign-in so you stay signed in.

Who handles it

We do not sell or share your information. It is held only by the services that run the site:

  • Vercel hosts the website and counts visits.
  • Supabase stores the email list, library accounts, and library files, in the United States.
  • Google provides "Sign in with Google" and the site's fonts.

Your choices

To see, correct, or delete anything we hold about you, write to jon@scios.tech.
